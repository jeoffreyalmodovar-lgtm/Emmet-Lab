About Me

Stepping onto the vibrant campus of the University of the Cordilleras (UC) as a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) student means entering a fast-paced world of innovation, logic, and endless problem-solving. Balancing the rich culture of Baguio City with the rigorous demands of a cutting-edge tech curriculum, this journey is defined by late-night coding sessions, collaborative triumphs, and continuous adaptation.

From day one, the academic experience is immersive. The curriculum transforms raw curiosity into technical expertise, guiding students through the complexities of computer programming, networking, web development, and database management. The challenge lies not just in writing code that works, but in understanding how technology bridges human needs and digital solutions. In UC’s modern computer laboratories, theory meets practice as lines of Java, Python, or C++ come to life, interspersed with the frantic clatter of keyboards and the shared relief of resolving a stubborn bug.

Beyond individual screens, the life of a UC BSIT student thrives on community. It is forged in the collaborative energy of capstone projects, hackathons, and student organizations like the Junior Information Technology Association (JITA). In these spaces, classmates become lifelines—sharing troubleshooting tips over a warm cup of coffee to brave the cold Baguio weather, and pushing each other to build smarter, more efficient systems.

As graduation approaches, the UC BSIT student emerges not just as a graduate, but as a resilient tech professional. Equipped with a solid technical foundation, sharp analytical skills, and the adaptive mindset required by an ever-evolving industry, they stand ready to step into the global workforce and shape the future of technology.

HTML Shopping Website Prototype


This project showcases the end-to-end conceptualization, design, and development of a modern e-commerce platform engineered to deliver a seamless, intuitive shopping experience. Driven by a user-centric approach, the application bridges the gap between complex digital retail architecture and fluid user interaction by integrating features like dynamic product filtering, a secure and streamlined checkout pipeline, real-time inventory management, and a fully responsive interface optimized for all devices. Beyond the visual aesthetics, the project focuses heavily on performance and scalability, utilizing optimized database queries, secure user authentication protocols, and clean, modular code to handle high-traffic browsing and ensure data integrity. The final product stands as a comprehensive demonstration of full-stack development, modern UI/UX principles, and the technical problem-solving required to build a secure, high-conversion digital storefront.


HTML RPG Game


This project features the development of an immersive, browser-based Role-Playing Game (RPG) engineered entirely using web technologies. Built to maximize the capabilities of native web architecture, the game integrates dynamic state management to track player progression, inventory systems, character stats, and turn-based combat logic seamlessly without relying on heavy external plugins. The project focuses heavily on clean, object-oriented JavaScript programming to handle procedural element generation, responsive enemy AI algorithms, and real-time event triggers, all while utilizing semantic HTML5 and advanced CSS for fluid, retro-inspired visual layouts and animations. By optimizing asset loading, rendering pipelines, and DOM performance, the final application delivers a highly responsive, lag-free gaming experience directly in the browser, serving as a powerful demonstration of interactive front-end engineering, game loop mechanics, and complex logic design.
